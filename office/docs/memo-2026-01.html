RE: Enforcement of Lineup and Anti-Competitive Bylaws During the 2026 "Jeremiah Smith Sweepstakes"

Dear Owners:

As the race for the 2027 1.01 heats up, the League Office has noticed some franchises taking "creative" approaches to roster management. This memo is a reminder that the Bylaws remain in full force. Tanking is a time-honored dynasty tradition. Rigging the system is not.

I. How Draft Order Works (Read This Twice)

Non-playoff draft order is set by Potential Points, the score your best possible lineup would have put up each week. It is not set by your record. Benching good players does nothing for you, and losing on purpose earns you nothing but shame.

II. Rules That Will Be Enforced

§ Lineups: "Each team must start players who are reasonably expected to play at least one snap in an NFL game that week."
§ Lineups: "Each owner is expected to make an honest attempt at starting their most optimal valid lineup."
§ Anti-Competitive Behavior: No owner may cut, drop, or dump "players who are obviously valuable."
§ Commissioner: The commissioner may act to stop "intentional sabotage of the league."

III. Effective Immediately

  1. Every starter must be a player expected to play that week. That means no IR, Out, PUP, Suspended, or bye-week players in your lineup.
  2. An empty bench is not an excuse. If your roster can't field a valid lineup, add healthy free agents.
  3. Stocking your roster with injured free agents to keep Potential Points low will be treated as intentional sabotage.
  4. The League Office will spot-check lineups at each Sunday's 1:00 PM ET kickoff.

IV. Penalties

1st offense: written warning (see your Sleeper inbox if applicable). 2nd offense: loss of your earliest owned rookie pick and all prize winnings for the season. 3rd offense: removal from the league. Yes, a franchise could tank all the way to the 1.01 and then lose it. That would be very funny for everyone except that franchise.

The League Office wishes every bottom-dweller good luck in the Jeremiah Smith Sweepstakes. Lose honestly.

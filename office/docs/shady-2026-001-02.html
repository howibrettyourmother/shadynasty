I. The Facts

Your response states: "I do not dispute the factual roster moves listed in Exhibit A." The facts are therefore established. The League Office appreciates your cooperation and has entered Exhibit A into the record as stipulated.

A. "Better than an empty slot." You argue that starting unavailable players is acceptable "when the alternative is an empty roster slot." The League Office respectfully notes that the free-agent pool has never been empty. It always contains healthy NFL players who are expected to take the field on Sunday. You had the option to add one. On October 21, 2026, you used that option five times, and each time you picked someone who could not play. The alternative was never an empty slot. It was a healthy free agent.

B. "Residual dynasty upside." Your response says several of the players "carry residual dynasty upside." The League Office does not dispute that a player on IR may be valuable in 2028. The Bylaws, however, do not ask about 2028:

§ Lineups: "Each team must start players who are reasonably expected to play at least one snap in an NFL game that week."

Upside is not a snap. Residual upside is still not a snap.

C. Potential Points. You correctly note that draft order is set by Potential Points "so that true team quality, rather than manufactured record, controls the order." The League Office agrees completely. Potential Points measures what your roster could score. Filling that roster with players who cannot score lowers the number on purpose. That is a manufactured result, which is the exact thing you just said the rule was written to prevent. The League Office thanks counsel for the citation.

§ Lineups: "Each owner is expected to make an honest attempt at starting their most optimal valid lineup for each week of the season."

II. Precedent

Your response says this league has "previously tolerated, and in some cases openly celebrated" similar conduct. It does not name one specific example. The League Office has done the research for you.

The League Office set aside every player who took the field and simply scored nothing. Showing up and doing nothing is a performance issue, not a Bylaws issue. That left the starts where the player never took the field at all. According to the official Sleeper records for this league, in the regular seasons of 2021 through 2025 (Weeks 1–14, with empty lineup slots, playoff and consolation weeks, and the current season not counted), that happened 47 times. In 43 of them the player was inactive, injured or not on an NFL team. In the other 4, the player's team was on a bye. No notices were issued for any of them.

Two of those 47 belong to a franchise operated by joebags85. Both came in Week 14 of 2023, when that franchise started C.J. Beathard and Aaron Jones and neither of them played. This office declined to issue a notice at the time. You may wish to update your exhibit list.

The League Office does not deny that those 47 starts happened. It denies that they were tanking. They are spread across twelve franchises over five seasons, at an average of fewer than one per franchise per season. That is what forgetting to check the injury report looks like. After reviewing the managers involved, this office finds that they were not trying to lose. They were simply bad at this. Incompetence is not a violation of the Bylaws. If it were, the league would have no members left and the League Office would have to issue a notice to itself.

The difference between those managers and you is intent. They made an honest attempt and failed. You made no attempt and succeeded. The record shows two such starts by your franchise in five full seasons. Exhibit A shows five unavailable players added on a single day in October. That is not a precedent. It is a new business model.

The League Office also notes that your response is signed "Co-Commissioner, SHADYNASTY League." You are claiming selective enforcement by an office that you co-run. If the League Office has singled you out, then you have, in part, singled out yourself. The League Office suggests you take this up with yourself at your earliest convenience.

On "prospective application." You ask that the standard "be applied prospectively and announced to the full league." The League Office graciously agrees. The standard applies from Week 7 onward. As it happens, Week 7 onward is exactly what you committed to in your own Section III. The League Office is pleased that the parties are in full agreement, and this letter is copied to the full league, so the standard has now been announced as well.

III. Consent Agreement

The League Office formally accepts the compliance commitments in your Section III and enters them as a binding Consent Agreement. Beginning in Week 7 and continuing through the end of the 2026 season, the Respondent shall:

  1. Start only players who are reasonably expected to play at least one snap that week, and add healthy free agents as needed;
  2. Stop acquiring injured players for the purpose of lowering Potential Points; and
  3. Operate the franchise in accordance with the written Bylaws.

Monitoring. The Respondent's compliance will be monitored through a Shoebeer Compliance Audit. Starting in Week 7, the Shoebeer Enforcement Division will review every Rebuilding Morehouse's lineup each week, looking specifically for any player whose injury status contains the letters "I" and "R." The Division will also look for any sign of a player expected to play a snap. Early findings suggest that search may take a while.

Penalty for Breach. Any breach of this Consent Agreement will be punished with a ceremonial shoey, to be performed on video and submitted to the court of jurisdiction. This is in addition to the remedies in the Owner Responsibilities penalty schedule. Notice SHADY-2026-001.01, including its status as your formal First Offense Warning, remains in full force and effect.

On the "Executive Council." You reserve the right to raise future concerns with "the full league or Executive Council if applicable." It is not applicable. The League Office has searched the Bylaws and confirms that this league has no Executive Council. The Executive Council is the group chat. It is also the court of jurisdiction, the appeals court and the press. Its rulings are final, binding, and usually accompanied by a GIF.

IV. Closing

This matter is resolved by consent. The file will remain open for monitoring through the end of the 2026 season. The League Office reads your response, which disputes none of the facts and agrees to every demand, as a plea of no contest. Nolo contendere accepted.
